HOW TO KILL A MONSTER

And Other Aspects of The Mother

Jenifer Hammond

I. A MONSTER HUNTERS METHODOLOGY

If you are intent on killing a monster, you must first know what a monster is. You can’t find a monster - even when subdued, let alone kill it, if you do not take the offered respite and bring first thy self into the light. For monsters are made real by a subjugated mind, such a mind exists and thrives, of only our own doing of false worshiping Aspects of the Mother with odes far too alike.

I’ve gathered some steps I expect will change as I go, as I’ve surveyed the ruins - ever despaired. Such destruction of beauty I’ve held without care.

Austere in my faith to purge all I am to abhor; the first step I followed - defining a monster to make a woman pure.
