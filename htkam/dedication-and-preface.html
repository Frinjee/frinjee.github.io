HOW TO KILL A MONSTER

And Other Aspects of The Mother

Jenifer Hammond

DEDICATION

To those I wounded and devoured with words of devotion, I offer myself a sacrificial atonement.

PREFACE

I began writing this as a means to subdue the Monster I had found after it devoured another, beautiful soul and destroyed a home I could have made.. And as I wrote, I thought, I felt, and so I wrote more. When I stumbled into reflections that evoked a feeling I had not known before, I kept writing, thinking, and feeling until I learned and fully knew that feeling was a start to forgiveness, of myself. A new method to take aim with, I had no longer had any doubt, that if I exposed all I had to forgive within, I could heal and give myself much more. And so I write, I think, I feel my reflections unembellished, furthermore very much unabridged to detail all provisions along the hunt to kill aspects of my Mother and the Monster, I found within.
